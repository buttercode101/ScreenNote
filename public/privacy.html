Privacy policy

ScreenNote — record, review and optionally share short screen videos with timestamped discussion. Android package za.co.buttercode.screennote.

Effective 26 October 2026.

1. Recording

Screen video is captured only after you approve Android's screen-capture consent dialog, and recording does not restart without fresh consent. If you switch the microphone on, your voice is recorded with the video. The app declares RECORD_AUDIO, POST_NOTIFICATIONS, FOREGROUND_SERVICE, FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MEDIA_PROJECTION and FOREGROUND_SERVICE_MICROPHONE so that a recording can continue behind a visible notification you can stop from. System audio and camera overlays are not captured in this version.

2. What stays on your device

Recordings, drafts and your recording library stay in the app's private storage until you choose to publish. Android cloud backup is switched off for this app (allowBackup="false").

3. What is uploaded, and only when you publish

When you explicitly publish a link, the video file and the details needed to serve it are uploaded: title, duration, size, expiry, and any password you set. Viewers can watch, react, and leave comments or replies, which are stored with the clip. A clip is limited to 5 minutes and 25 MB, and the service runs on a shared 1 GB storage budget.

4. Names and access are not verified

Display names and reviewer identities used in discussions are not verified. Creator and author controls rely on private capability links held by whoever created the content, not on a verified account. A displayed name is not proof of identity.

5. Retention and deletion

Published clips expire after the window the creator chooses — 1, 3 or 7 days (7 days by default). When a creator stops sharing or deletes a published clip, media links already handed out may keep working for up to about five minutes, because links are signed to be valid for that long. Deleting the local recording does not delete a published copy; stop sharing or delete the published clip to do that. Expired, deleted and unclaimed uploads are reclaimed by the service's retention process. The app runs on third-party hosting, which keeps ordinary request logs such as IP address and browser information for its own default retention period; the app does not add analytics of its own.

6. What you record, and content responsibility

You are responsible for what you record and publish, and for having the right to record and share it, including anything visible on your screen. Reviewers' comments are stored with the clip. This build has no in-app report button for a viewer; if content shows you and you want it removed, contact the person who shared the link, or raise it through the contact route in section 10.

7. Security

Uploaded content is stored in private cloud storage and served through time-limited signed links. Password protection for a shared link is optional and chosen by the creator.

8. Children

ScreenNote is a work and product-feedback tool and is not directed at children.

9. Your control

You can delete local recordings at any time, and stop sharing or delete a published clip to remove it from the server. Requests to remove content that shows you: see section 10.

10. Contact

Privacy questions or removal requests: open an issue at github.com/buttercode101/ScreenNote/issues, or use the developer contact address shown on the app's Google Play listing.

11. Changes

If these practices change, this page is updated and carries a new effective date.
