1. Recording

Screen video is captured only through Android's screen-capture consent dialog, which you must approve. The app does not restart recording without new consent. If you enable the microphone, your voice is recorded with the video. This app declares microphone access, notification access, and the foreground-service permissions needed for media projection and microphone so a recording can continue with a visible, stoppable notification. System audio and camera overlays are not included in this version.

2. What stays on your device

Recordings, drafts and the recording library stay in the app's private storage until you choose to publish. Android cloud backup is disabled for this app.

3. What is uploaded, and only when you publish

When you explicitly publish a link, the video file and the details needed to serve it are uploaded: title, duration, size, expiry and any password you set. Viewers can watch, react, and leave comments or replies, which are stored with the clip. Uploads are limited to 5 minutes and 25 MB, within a shared 1 GB storage budget.

4. Names and access

Display names and reviewer identities used in discussions are not verified. Creator and author controls rely on private capabilities held by the person who created the content, not on a verified account. A displayed name is not proof of identity.

5. Retention and deletion

Published clips expire after the 1, 3 or 7 days you choose. When you stop sharing or delete a published clip, previously issued media links may keep working for up to about five minutes. Deleting the local recording does not delete a published copy — stop sharing or delete the published clip to do that.
